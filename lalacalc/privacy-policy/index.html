Privacy Policy

LalaCalc: Graphing Calculator

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Android app version 1.0

LalaCalc performs its built-in calculations and Python execution on your device. Crow Game Studio does not receive your calculation inputs, saved programs, or preferences through the app. Android backups, voluntary support emails, and visits to this policy are explained below.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Crow Game Studio publishes LalaCalc: Graphing Calculator for Android (package name com.lalacalc). This policy describes how the app handles information, and what happens when you contact us or visit this policy page.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact ejherran.c@gmail.com.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Calculations and working content. Expressions, results, graphs, equations, linear systems, statistics, and Python program execution are processed locally to provide the features you use.
  • Saved programs. Python programs you choose to save are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Preferences. Calculation and display settings, guide language, and custom function-key assignments are stored locally so the app can remember your choices.

LalaCalc does not require an account. Version 1.0 does not request Android's Internet permission and does not include advertising, analytics, or a separate crash-reporting SDK. Crow Game Studio does not collect or sell your calculation inputs, saved programs, or preferences through the app.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

3. Clipboard and programs you run

When you use a copy action, the selected result or program text is placed on the Android system clipboard. Clipboard access and any later use of that text by your keyboard or another app are governed by Android and those apps.

Python code you choose to run executes on your device with the access Android permits to LalaCalc. Its effects on local files depend on that code. Crow Game Studio does not receive the contents of your programs through the app.

4. Android backup and device transfers

This release enables Android backup. Depending on your device, operating system, backup provider, and settings, app data such as saved programs and preferences may be included in cloud backups or transfers to another device.

These services are operated by Android, Google, or your device's backup provider. Crow Game Studio does not operate or access your Android backups. You can manage backups through your device and backup-account settings. Existing backups may be retained or restored independently of deleting the app's local data.

For Google's services, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Support and platform diagnostics

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, and maintain the app. Email is handled by our email provider, Gmail, and your email provider under their respective policies.

Google Play and Android may process installation information and diagnostic reports under their own settings and policies. Google Play may make app-quality statistics and crash or application-not-responding reports available to us through Play Console. We may use those reports to investigate reliability issues. LalaCalc does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it to third parties.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

Saved programs and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or clear the app's storage. You can delete individual programs in the app. Clearing LalaCalc's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it removes its local app data. Android backups and content you have copied elsewhere may remain under the controls described above.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain a record of its resolution, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of information you sent us by emailing ejherran.c@gmail.com. We cannot delete backups or copies controlled by your device, backup provider, or another app.

Local files and preferences use Android's app-private storage and rely on Android's access controls and your device's security settings. Crow Game Studio does not operate a server that stores your calculations or programs. No account-deletion process is needed because LalaCalc does not create user accounts.

7. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and may process other technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website activity is separate from calculations performed in LalaCalc.

Crow Game Studio has not added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, or third-party embeds to this page.

8. Children's privacy

LalaCalc's built-in features do not require accounts or send personal information to Crow Game Studio. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support email, contact us so we can address the request and delete that information when appropriate.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy. Material changes to the app's handling of information will be reflected in this policy and, where needed, in the app.
